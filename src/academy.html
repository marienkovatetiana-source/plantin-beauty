FAQ

Academy questions

Do I need any experience?

No. The courses start from the basics, so complete beginners are welcome. If you already work in beauty, tell us and we will adjust the pace.

Are materials included?

Yes, everything you need during the course. Afterwards you can buy a ready starter kit at the studio or get our list of recommended materials and shops.

Is the certificate recognised?

You receive a European-format certificate when you finish. Rules for working in beauty differ by country, so ask us if you are unsure.

Can I pay in instalments?

Full payment is due before the course starts. If that is a problem, write to us on WhatsApp and we will look for a solution.

What if I can’t come?

Cancellation is free up to 48 h before the course. Message us and we will move you to the next group.
