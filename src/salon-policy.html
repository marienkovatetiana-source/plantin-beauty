Salon policy

When you book, a master keeps that time only for you. These simple rules keep it fair for you, for other clients and for our team.

Cancelling

Cancelling or changing

Why? A last-minute free slot almost never gets filled, and your master is paid for that time either way.

Rule Treatments Courses
Free cancellation up to 24 h before up to 48 h before
Late cancellation less than 24 h: 50% of the price —
No-show, no message 100% of the price —
Payment at the studio in full before the course starts

How fees are charged

A late-cancellation or no-show fee is added to your next booking. If you have no next booking, we send an invoice.

Prepayment

Prepaying online through Treatwell is optional. After a no-show without a reason, prepayment becomes required for your next bookings.

Running late?

Message us on WhatsApp. If there’s not enough time for the full treatment, we’ll suggest a shorter one or a new time.

Guarantee

7-day guarantee

If your nails chip or lift, or your lashes come off, within 7 days of your visit, we fix it for free. Just message us a photo. After 7 days, corrections are charged separately.

The guarantee doesn’t cover

  • Damage from a knock or accident
  • Removing or picking off the work yourself
  • Not following the aftercare advice for permanent make-up or facials

Questions about the rules?

Message or call us — we’re happy to explain.
