FAQ

Lashes & Brows questions

How long do lash extensions last?

Usually 3–4 weeks, with a refill every 2–3 weeks to keep them full.

Can I wear mascara?

We recommend not to. Mascara and oil-based products shorten how long extensions last.

Is lamination the same as extensions?

No. Lamination lifts and sets your own lashes or brows; extensions add length and volume.

Can I sleep on my side?

Yes. After the first 24 hours you can sleep as usual.

Can I book lashes and brows together?

Yes. Write to us on WhatsApp and we will find a time for both.
