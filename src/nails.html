FAQ

Nails questions

How long does gel polish last?

Usually 3–4 weeks. Then come back for a refill or removal — please don’t remove gel at home.

When should I come back for a refill?

Usually after 3–4 weeks, when the nails have grown out. Book a refill, not gel polish — it takes longer.

Which materials do you use?

Professional gel systems and tools that are sterilised after every client. Ask your master if you have an allergy.

Is gel bad for my nails?

Not when it is applied and removed properly. Don’t pick or peel it, and let us remove it safely.

Can I book two treatments in one visit?

Yes, for example manicure and pedicure. Write to us on WhatsApp and we will find a time for both.
