Usually every 4–6 weeks, depending on your skin and goals.

A little redness can happen and usually fades within a few hours.

Yes. Tell us about your skin when you book and we will choose gentle products.

Can I wear make-up afterwards?

It is better to let your skin rest for the rest of the day.
