FAQ

Permanent make-up questions

How long does permanent make-up last?

Usually 1–3 years, depending on skin type and aftercare. A refresh keeps the colour.

Does it hurt?

We use numbing, so most clients feel only light pressure.

How long does it take to heal?

About 4 weeks. Colour looks stronger at first and softens as it heals.

Is a touch-up included?

Yes. One touch-up after healing is part of the price.

Who should not get it?

Pregnant or breastfeeding clients, and some skin conditions. Ask us if you are not sure.
